Conclusion

ILIA x CENIA

I thought it was a very interesting talk about why we should create our own technology instead of just consuming it. The problem with using foreign artificial intelligence is that it can bring biases or fail to understand us properly. That's why we need sovereignty and to build our own model. If we can get universities to work together with companies, improve our teams, and protect our data right here, we will be able to create a completely independent artificial intelligence for ourselves.

Game UI Conference

The conference on video game interfaces was very engaging. I was surprised by the amount of detail required in everyday software. I also found it interesting that the way we organize and adapt a design is essential to user comfort. An interface is not just about looking good; it shapes the entire experience of an application.
